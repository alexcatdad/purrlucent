New `pl-accordion` and `pl-accordion-item`: stacked disclosure sections, each a heading with a button that shows and hides its panel. One section is open at a time unless `multiple` is set. Enter and Space toggle a section, and Up, Down, Home and End move between headers. `level` sets the heading level, and `pl-toggle` reports `{ value, open }`.
