`pl-button` takes `size="sm"`, a 24px control for dense tables, toolbars and pagination, with a new `--pl-size-sm` token; a coarse pointer keeps the 44px hit region. A `type="submit"` button now submits on Enter in a native text input of its form, also with several fields. `pl-button` gains `form`, `name` and `value`: a submit button outside its `<form>` submits the form named by `form`, and its `name` and `value` are submitted when it submits, as a native submit button's are. The React types include the three.
