New `pl-month-range-picker`: chooses a range of whole months, one year at a time in a three-column grid, as a button with a popover or `inline`. The first month starts the range and the second ends it, in either order; leaving after one month chooses that month alone. The value is `yyyy-mm/yyyy-mm`, it takes part in forms, and month names follow the locale. New message keys: `chooseMonths`, `previousYear`, `nextYear` and `labelValue`.
