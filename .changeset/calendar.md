New `pl-calendar`, a month calendar for choosing one day, as an accessible date grid. Arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down move through the days, and Enter or Space selects. `min`, `max` and `disabled-dates` (or a `disabledDates` function) limit the choice. Month and weekday names, the first weekday and the direction follow the locale. The value is an ISO day, `yyyy-mm-dd`, and a change fires `pl-change` with `{ value }`.
