New `pl-resizable` split view with `pl-resizable-panel` and `pl-resizable-handle`. Panels sit side by side or stacked, with sizes in percent held within each panel's `min` and `max`. People drag the dividers, or use the arrow, Home, End and Enter keys on them, following the window splitter pattern. Enter collapses a `collapsible` panel and restores it, and the `collapsed` attribute does the same from your own controls. `pl-resize` reports the sizes.
