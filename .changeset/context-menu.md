Adds `pl-context-menu`, which wraps a region and opens a slotted `pl-menu` at the pointer on a right-click or Control-click, at the finger after a long press, and from the keyboard with Shift+F10 or the Context Menu key. It reports `pl-open` and `pl-close`, and a `disabled` region leaves the browser's own menu alone.
