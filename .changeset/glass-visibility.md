Glass is easier to see. Glass surfaces use a new `--pl-material-glass` fill, more translucent than before (64% in light, 74% in dark), with more color boost (`--pl-material-saturate`, 160%). The install guide says glass needs varied content behind it and shows a background that gives it some, and how to tell when the system's reduced-transparency setting is keeping it solid.
