Glass picks up more color from what is behind it: `--pl-material-saturate` raises the boost from 125% to 160%. Glass surfaces read their fill from a new `--pl-material-glass` token, which stays as opaque as text on glass needs over any backdrop; an app that controls what sits behind its glass may set it clearer. The install guide says glass needs varied content behind it, shows a background that gives it some, and how to tell when the system's reduced-transparency setting is keeping it solid.
