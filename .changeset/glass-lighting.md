Glass catches light. Every glass surface draws a brighter specular line along its top rim, a glow inside the top edge and a faint catch light along the bottom rim, through `--pl-material-edge` and the new `--pl-material-glow` and `--pl-material-rim-low` tokens. Glass controls cast a contact shadow and a softer lifting one. The fills are unchanged, so text contrast is the same as before.
