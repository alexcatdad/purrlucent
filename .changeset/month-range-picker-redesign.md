`pl-month-range-picker` has a new look. Its trigger is a field like `pl-select`, with a calendar icon, a chevron, a placeholder and a `slot="label"` label. The chosen range shows as one band with filled end caps, a preview of the range follows the pointer and keyboard focus after the first month, and this month is marked with a dot. The new `presets` property adds buttons that choose ready-made ranges.
