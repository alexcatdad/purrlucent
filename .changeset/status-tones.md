Success and warning tones for status interfaces. New tokens: `--pl-color-success`, `--pl-color-success-foreground`, `--pl-color-success-text` and the same three for `warning`. `pl-badge` gains `success` and `warning` variants and a `dot` attribute for status pills. `pl-progress` gains `tone` (`success`, `warning`, `destructive`). `pl-banner` gains `success` and `warning` tones and `no-close`. `pl-toggle-group` gains `fit-content`, for items as wide as their text in a group that scrolls sideways when it overflows.
