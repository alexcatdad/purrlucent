`pl-collapsible` accepts a slotted `button`, `a[href]` or `[role="button"]` as `slot="trigger"`: it replaces the built-in button and gets `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and toggling on click, Enter and Space. A new `current` attribute marks and fills the row like a current sidebar row. `pl-tabs` has a `hide-scrollbar` attribute that hides the scrollbar of an overflowing tab list while it stays scrollable.
