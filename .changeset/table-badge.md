`pl-table` styles interactive rows (a `data-interactive` row or one with an `a[data-stretched]` link gets a hover tint, a focus ring and a pointer) and sortable column headers (a `th[aria-sort]` holding a button shows a sort glyph that follows `aria-sort`). `pl-badge` gains an `appearance` attribute: `solid` (default), `soft` or `outline`.
