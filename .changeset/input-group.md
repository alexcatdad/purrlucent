New `pl-input-group` wraps a `pl-input` or `pl-textarea` with addons before and after it, as `slot="start"` and `slot="end"`: text, an svg icon or a `pl-button`. The field and its addons share one border and one focus ring, and the field keeps its own label, name, value, validation and events. Text in an addon can describe the field when the field's `aria-describedby` names the addon's `id`.
