Taller controls. The default control height is 32px, up from 24px, and `lg` and `xl` are 36px and 40px, up from 28px and 36px. Buttons, fields, selects, segmented controls, tabs and the other controls follow, as does the fine-pointer hit size. Bars and toolbars grow by 4px with `xl`. A coarse pointer still gets at least 44px.

The switch keeps its own track heights (24, 28 and 36px) and is now always 2.25 times as wide as it is tall, so its thumb has room to travel at any size.
