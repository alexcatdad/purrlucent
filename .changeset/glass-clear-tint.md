Clear glass and tint. `pl-glass="clear"` on a container inside glass gives its controls Apple's clear variant for photos and video: a dark film over a dimmed backdrop, with light text in both color schemes, solid again under reduced transparency or more contrast. `--pl-material-tint` and `--pl-material-tint-strength` mix a color into the glass fill. The Surfaces stories show both, plus a prototype of edge lensing through an SVG filter, which only Chromium renders.
