Adds `pl-chart`, a named, themed chart drawn by TanStack Charts (an optional peer dependency). Give it a `type` (`bar`, `line`, `area` or `donut`), `data` and the keys to read, or a TanStack `definition`. It has a required `label`, a tooltip and arrow-key navigation by default, series colors from `--pl-chart-1` to `--pl-chart-6`, an optional legend, and a native table of the same values for assistive technology that `show-table` makes visible.
