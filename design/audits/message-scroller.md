# HIG audit: message scroller

Source: [HIG Scroll views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/scroll-views) (change log: June 8, 2026) and [HIG Generative AI](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/generative-ai), read 2026-11-23. Code: `src/components/message-scroller/`. Verdicts: **met**, **gap**, **deviation** (deliberate, with a reason), or **needs eyes**. Rows marked "not stated on the HIG page" are web behavior. Scroll edge effects, page-by-page scrolling, zoom and the platform sections that do not apply to a scrolling transcript are not audited. The Generative AI page has no scrolling or streaming guidance; it asks for feedback while a reply is generated, which the `busy` state serves.

## Message scroller (`pl-message-scroller`, `pl-message-scroller-item`, `pl-message-scroller-button`)

### Library guidelines

| Guideline (HIG wording, shortened)                                 | Verdict    | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Support default scrolling gestures and keyboard shortcuts.         | met        | The host is the native scroller (`overflow-y: auto`); nothing moves by transform. E2E: "scrolling is native: the keyboard scrolls the focused region".                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A scrolling region can be reached and scrolled with the keyboard.  | met        | The host is a region with `tabindex="0"`. E2E: "the host is a named region and a tab stop".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Make it apparent when content is scrollable.                       | needs eyes | The edges fade where there is more to see, from tokens, and `data-scrollable` mirrors the state. E2E: "the edges fade where there is more to see". Whether the fade reads well on every material was not looked at.                                                                                                                       |
| Small scroll bars suit tight spaces.                               | met        | `scrollbar-width: thin` with the thumb and track tokens, as `pl-scroll-area`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| The view scrolls only as far as needed to bring content into view. | deviation  | A `scroll-anchor` row goes to the top with a peek, not "nearest". That is the point of the pattern: the question stays above its reply. `scrollToMessage` takes `align: "nearest"` for the minimal move.                                                                                                                                  |
| Do not move content under the reader.                              | met        | Nothing scrolls unless asked: `auto-scroll` is off by default, it follows only at the end, and wheel, touch, scroll keys, the scrollbar and a text selection let go of it. Older rows added before the first keep the visible rows in place. E2E in "auto-scroll" and "placement".                                                        |
| (Platform behavior) Named region.                                  | met        | `label` (default "Messages") gives `role="region"` and `aria-label`; the log inside is `role="log"` with `aria-relevant="additions"`, and `aria-busy` while a reply streams. E2E: "label names the region and no label drops the role", "the log announces additions and is busy only while a reply streams". Not stated on the HIG page. |
| (Platform behavior) Reduced motion.                                | met        | A smooth scroll becomes a jump, the edge fades never animate, and the button's fade-in uses `--pl-duration`, which is 0 under reduced motion. E2E: "reduced motion makes a smooth scroll a jump", "the fades do not animate".                                                                                                             |
| (Platform behavior) Forced colors and increased contrast.          | met        | Forced colors draw no mask and use system scrollbar and focus colors; increased contrast takes the stronger thumb color. E2E: "forced colors draw no mask", "the focus ring is visible under forced colors".                                                                                                                              |
| (Platform behavior) The button is a large enough target.           | met        | The button is a `pl-button` icon button, which carries `--pl-size-hit-min`. E2E: "the scroll-to-end button is at least 44 by 44".                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| (Platform behavior) Focus is not lost when the button goes away.   | met        | Pressing the button moves focus to the scroller, because the button becomes inert at the end. E2E: "after it is pressed, focus moves to the scroller".                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| (Platform behavior) An empty button is not left in the tab order.  | met        | The button is `inert` and transparent while nothing is below. E2E: "it is inert when there is nothing to scroll to".                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| (Platform behavior) Right-to-left.                                 | met        | Logical properties throughout; the button is centered. E2E: "the button sits at the center under rtl and the region scrolls".                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| (Platform behavior) Text can grow to 200%.                         | met        | E2E: "no horizontal overflow at 200% text zoom".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

### Usage guidelines (for `docs/components.md`, not the component)

- Give the scroller a size, such as `block-size` or `max-block-size`; without one it grows with its rows and never scrolls.
- Set `busy` while a reply streams and clear it when the reply ends, so assistive technology reads the finished reply once.
- A row marked `scroll-anchor` cannot reach the top while the content below it is shorter than the viewport. The view holds it at the end until the reply is long enough, then keeps it at the peek; with `auto-scroll` it follows the end instead once the reply fills the viewport.
- The element never parses message text; render markdown in the row.
- Do not nest message scrollers.

## Not verified

- Nothing was run in a browser; the e2e results above are read from the test files, not executed.
- No virtual screen reader test was written; the roles are on the host and the shadow log, and the exact spoken phrases were not confirmed.
- Scrollbar-drag release and smooth-scroll animation timing were not tested.
- The HIG pages were read through their published JSON on 2026-11-23; their images were not viewed.
