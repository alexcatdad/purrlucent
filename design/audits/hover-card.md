# HIG audit: hover card

Source: [HIG Popovers](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/popovers). The HIG has no hover card page; the hover card is a popover that opens on hover and focus, so the popover guidelines apply and the popover audit (`popover-tooltip.md`) covers the shared look. Code: `src/components/hover-card/`, `src/components/popover/popover.css.ts`, `src/behaviors/anchored.ts`, `src/behaviors/dismiss.ts`, `src/behaviors/position.ts`.

Verdicts: **met**, **gap**, **deviation** (deliberate, with a reason), or **needs eyes** (a visual judgement for the maintainer). Rows marked "not stated on the HIG page" are platform behavior or web accessibility practice. Nothing was run for this audit: the e2e tests cited were read, not executed.

## Usage guidelines (for `docs/components.md`, not the component)

- Use a hover card to preview what a link or control points at, such as a profile or a repository. Do not put the only way to do something in it: a pointer-only or keyboard-only user may never open it, and a touch user needs a tap that stops the trigger from acting first.
- Keep the content to a small amount of information, as for a popover (popovers/1). Use a popover when the content is needed on demand and holds the task.
- Do not put a warning in a hover card (popovers/11).
- Give the card a `label` when its content does not name itself.
- Prefer a trigger whose own action also gives the content, such as a link to the page the card previews, so the first-tap behavior on touch costs one extra tap, not a lost feature.

## Findings outside HIG

- The 700ms open and 300ms close delays are fixed times in `hover-card.css.ts`, overridable with `--pl-hover-card-open-delay` and `--pl-hover-card-close-delay`. They are not motion tokens, so they stay under reduced motion. The values follow common web hover cards; the HIG gives none.
- Not verified: a hover card nested inside a popover or another hover card, a disabled trigger, and a trigger that is not focusable.
