# HIG audit: drawer (bottom sheets and side panels)

Source: [HIG Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets) (iOS and iPadOS sheets, the grabber, detents, swipe to dismiss) and [HIG Sidebars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars) / [Split views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/split-views) for the side panel. Code: `src/components/drawer/` (`pl-drawer`, `detents.ts`), `hig/rules/sheets.yaml`. Counterpart of shadcn's Drawer and Sheet. `pl-dialog` is not modified and not imported; the drawer uses `src/behaviors/slot-presence.ts` and the same native `<dialog>` approach.

Verdicts: **met**, **gap**, **deviation** (deliberate, with a reason), **needs eyes** (a visual judgement for the maintainer). Not read in a browser: this audit is from the code, and every "needs eyes" row is unverified.

## Deviations

- **No `top` side.** HIG shows sheets rising from the bottom of the screen (iOS) and panels at the sides; no HIG pattern slides in from the top. Not built.
- **Side panels are modal.** HIG says supplementary content that affects the main task belongs in a nonmodal panel or inspector (sheets/8). A modal side panel is the right shape for a settings or detail sheet over a page; a nonmodal inspector is the job of a split view or `pl-sidebar`, not of this component.
- **Dragging only on a bottom sheet.** Side panels close by the backdrop, Escape or an action; they are not dragged.

## Usage guidelines (for `docs/components.md`)

- Use a bottom sheet for a short, scoped task on a small screen, and a side panel for details or settings beside a wide page. Use `pl-dialog` for a centered sheet or an alert.
- Show one drawer at a time; close one before opening another (sheets/6).
- Give the drawer a `title`; add a `description` when the title is not enough. Without a title set `label`.
- Set `dismissible` only when pressing outside may discard state, and handle `pl-cancel` to confirm when work would be lost.
- Offer a Cancel or Done action in the `footer` with `data-close`; Escape and the grabber are not enough for everyone.
- Give `detents` only for content that suits two heights, such as a list that opens at half height.

## Findings outside HIG

- The scrim color expression is copied from `dialog.css.ts` because it is not a token. A `--pl-color-scrim` token would remove the copy.
- The scroll lock duplicates `pl-dialog`'s idea with its own counter and an attribute on the root, so a dialog and a drawer open together cannot leave the page locked.
