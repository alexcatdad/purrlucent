# HIG audit: toast

Source: [HIG Notifications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/notifications) and [HIG Alerts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts), rules in `hig/rules/notifications.yaml` and `hig/rules/alerts.yaml`. HIG has no toast page. A toast is the nearest HIG form of a non-blocking status message: it follows the Notifications content guidance, and the rows marked "not stated on HIG" are platform behavior and WAI-ARIA practice, unverified against Apple's own controls. Code: `src/components/toast/`, `src/components/button/`, `src/behaviors/slot-presence.ts`, `src/behaviors/glyphs.ts`, `src/surfaces/surfaces.css.ts`. Nothing was run for this audit: no tests, no Storybook, no browser; the verdicts come from reading the source, stories and e2e files.

Verdicts: **met**, **gap**, **deviation** (deliberate, with a reason), or **needs eyes** (a visual judgement for the maintainer).

## Usage guidelines (for `docs/components.md`, not the component)

- Place one `pl-toaster` early in the page and leave it there, so the live region exists before the first toast is added. `PlToaster.show()` makes one when there is none, and then waits 100 ms before adding the toast.
- Use a toast for the result of something the person just did ("Saved", "Copied"). Do not use it for an error that blocks them or for news they must act on (use a dialog or an inline message), and put nothing in it that they must read in a hurry or that is private (notifications/2, notifications/5).
- Give a short title that says what happened, and at most one sentence of description (notifications/1). Name an action with a verb ("Undo", "View") that does what it says.
- Show one toast per event; keep the returned element to close it when it goes stale (notifications/4).
- An action that undoes something should keep working elsewhere in the page: the toast may be gone.

## Findings outside HIG

- Roles: inside the toaster a destructive toast is its own `role="alert"` within the polite `role="status"` region. Some screen readers may announce it twice. Unverified without a screen reader run.
- `PlToaster.show()` with no toaster waits 100 ms so the new live region is registered before the toast arrives; the delay is a guess.
- The toaster is a manual popover for the top layer. A modal dialog makes everything outside it inert, so a toast shown while a dialog is open can be seen but not pressed until the dialog closes. Unverified.
- There is no limit on how many toasts stack.
- Time pauses only on hover and focus; a hidden tab keeps counting.
